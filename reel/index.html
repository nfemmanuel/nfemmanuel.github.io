reel · prototype
A 3-minute test

Job applications ask for the same seven things. Every. Single. Time.

Name, email, portfolio link, "why us?", resume, headshot, phone. Reel is a phone app that keeps everything you reuse one gesture away — inside whatever app you're filling. This page fakes two job applications so you can feel it.

🎛️The dialA control docked to the screen edge — the Android experience. One-finger gestures, clicks you can feel. ⌨️The keyboardYour stuff inside the keyboard — the iPhone experience. Search and tap.

Before you start

The vault comes preloaded as Alex Rivera, a demo applicant. Anything you add stays in this browser only — nothing uploads, nothing leaves your device.

The real product inserts into other apps — Safari forms, Gmail, LinkedIn. A webpage can't reach outside itself, so judge the speed and the feel here, not the reach.

Test 1 · the dial

Apply to Northbeam using only the dial →

The round button on the right edge is the app. It floats over whatever you're doing.

Three moves

Tap the round buttonOpens and stays. Spin a ring — it steps one item at a time and settles; the item at the red mark is selected. Tap it to insert into the box you last touched.
Hold · slide out · releaseOne finger, never lifting. Hold the button, then slide out: a little reaches the inner ring (collections), further reaches the outer ring (items). Arc your finger to spin whichever you're on — the lit ring is the one you're holding. Release on the outer ring to insert and put it away.
Double-tap the buttonPastes your most recent item instantly. No dial.
Pins & slow-cycleThe two edge slots are pinned 📌 — same place forever, tap to use. Hold a finger on any off-center item and the ring cycles slowly on its own until you let go.

Inner ring = collections. Outer ring = what's inside. The rings step and settle — no half-turns. Sound on 🔊 for the clicks.

＋ Make it yours

Add your own resume or a photo — it appears in the dial's Docs/Images and you can attach it above. The file never uploads; only its name is remembered, in your browser.

Also: select any text on this page and a Save to Reel chip appears — write one line of your own cover letter, select it, save it, reuse it on the keyboard page.
