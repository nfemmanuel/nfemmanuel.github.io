The keyboard — an Apple replica with one difference

review prototype

Type on it. Hold a vowel. Double-tap shift. Hold the space bar. Everything here is meant to be indistinguishable from the iPhone keyboard except the strip along the top — that part is ours. Turn on X-ray to see which parts are whose.

Two keyboards, both current. Apple ships the flat one and the glass one at the same time and picks between them per app, so this is not a question of which year it is. The page opens on the flat one because that is what your capture shows. Suggested typing: Apple's word suggestions are its own keyboard's and are lent to nobody — but the band they sit in is the band our strip uses, so switching the second control shows you the trade at true size.
