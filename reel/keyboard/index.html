The keyboard: an Apple replica with one difference

review prototype

Type on it. Hold a vowel. Double-tap shift. Hold the space bar. Everything here is meant to be indistinguishable from the iPhone keyboard except the small mark at the top left, which is ours. Tap it and your things come up over the keys. Turn on X-ray to see which parts are whose.

The row beside the mark. It fills with things you saved as you type past the third letter of one, and it stays empty the rest of the time, which is what your phone's own row does before it has anything to say. Press Play a line to watch it happen, or type resu or head yourself. Two keyboards, both current: Apple ships the flat one and the glass one at the same time and picks between them per app, so this is not a question of which year it is. The page opens on the flat one because that is what your capture shows.
